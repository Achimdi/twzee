      Questions

      Good to know

      Can't find your answer? Chat with us.

      How do I place an order?

      Add items from the menu, open your order, choose delivery or pickup and add your details. Press Send my order and your order opens in WhatsApp, already written out. Press send there and we confirm it with you.

      How do I pay?

      No payment is taken on this website. When we confirm your order on WhatsApp, we tell you the final amount and how to pay.

      Where do you deliver, and how much is it?

      We deliver within town for a flat ₦1,500. Delivery is free when your items total ₦25,000 or more, and pickup is always free.

      When are you open?

      See our opening hours in the Delivery section. You can send an order at any time, and we confirm it when we open.

      Can you cater for my event?

      Yes. Build a custom food tray from the menu, or chat with us for event packs and pricing for large orders.
